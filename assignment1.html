Scenario

The Multiplayer Synthesizer scenario challenges the conventional assumption that digital musical instruments are primarily designed for individual users. I interpret this scenario as an opportunity to explore how a browser-based instrument can make musical creation a shared and collaborative experience between two or more people using the same computer. Rather than requiring users to have prior musical knowledge or experience with synthesizers, the instrument should provide a low-barrier way for participants to experiment, respond to one another and contribute to a shared sound. Its purpose is to allow users to create something new and enjoyable through collaboration, where musical expression emerges from the interaction between participants rather than from individual technical skill. The worth of the project lies in creating an inclusive, beginner-friendly and socially engaging creative experience, where people with different levels of musical experience can participate meaningfully and share creative agency.

Responses

The proposed project is a cooperative browser-based synthesizer designed for two or more users to create music together. The instrument will distribute complementary forms of control between participants, encouraging users to listen, respond and experiment with each other's input rather than simply operating independent instruments. Collaboration will therefore become an essential part of the musical interaction, with the shared sound emerging from the combined actions of the participants. The interface will avoid the complexity of conventional synthesizers by presenting a limited set of expressive and understandable controls that can be explored without prior musical knowledge. This approach responds to the scenario's purpose by transforming music-making into a shared, playful and exploratory activity, while its value is established through inclusive participation, low barriers to entry and social engagement. The project aims to demonstrate that meaningful musical expression can emerge from cooperation rather than technical expertise.

Framing

The project will be framed around three interconnected principles: simplified interaction, shared control and collaborative creative agency. The interface will use a simplified UI to reduce the cognitive and technical barriers commonly associated with synthesizers, focusing on a small number of expressive controls rather than reproducing the complexity of professional music production software. Controls will be distributed between participants so that each user can influence a complementary aspect of the sound, while the resulting audio remains part of a shared musical system. A split-screen layout, divided control areas or multiple browser windows may be explored as possible interface structures for supporting simultaneous participation; the final spatial arrangement will be informed by interaction design research and testing rather than being predetermined. The project will focus on local, simultaneous and playful interaction, with experimentation and communication between participants forming part of the instrument's characterisation. It will exclude complex music-production workflows, advanced synthesis parameters, individual user accounts and remote online multiplayer functionality. This framing positions the project as a collaborative creative instrument rather than a conventional synthesizer, with the interface designed to encourage experimentation, shared ownership and accessible participation.
